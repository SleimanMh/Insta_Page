Each direction below is shown as a live homepage hero, plus the palette, type and layout rules that would carry it through Services, Work, About, Contact and the Quotation Builder. Layout & signature

  • The signal lineA 1px trace that runs between sections and pulses when you scroll into a new stage. Services, process and the quote builder all hang off it.
  • Blueprint gridA visible 56px grid in hero areas only, faded out with a mask. Content sits on it like a technical drawing.
  • Readouts, not iconsCredibility shown as live-looking telemetry (commit hashes, uptime, response times) rather than stock icon rows.
  • Asymmetric 12-colHeadline left, supporting text bottom-aligned right. Wide display type, generous negative space.
Services

A vertical pipeline. Each service is a node; expanding it shows deliverables, problems solved and stack. Packages are drawn as routes that light up several nodes at once.

Work

Case studies as "build logs": a large mockup, then challenge, solution and result as timestamped entries with real metrics.

Quote builder

The configurator is a schematic. Each selection adds a node and a cost line to a running "bill of materials" panel that stays docked on the right.

Watch out for

Can drift cold or too "developer-only". Countered with warm case-study photography and human copy on About.

My call

Go with Signal, borrow one idea from Module

Signal fits Innovative's positioning best. Your core message is that you design, develop, deploy and maintain. Signal is the only direction where that sentence is the visual system itself: the lifecycle line connects every page, and the quotation builder becomes a transparent bill of materials that grows as clients configure.

From Module, I would borrow real UI fragments as proof on the homepage and portfolio, so the site shows actual product craft and avoids feeling like a pure developer tool. Prism's glass and gradients would be held back to a single moment: the final call to action.

What happens after you choose

  1. Design system sheet: tokens, type scale, spacing, components and motion rules.
  2. Homepage at desktop, tablet and mobile.
  3. Services, Work, case study, About and Contact.
  4. Quotation builder: all four steps plus the summary, with working interactions and indicative pricing.
  5. Self-review pass, then a review link for you.
